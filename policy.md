## Return window
Refunds are available for 30 days after delivery. After 30 days the agent cannot refund; the case goes to a human.

## Refund amount
A refund can never be more than the item price the customer paid. One refund per order; anything further goes to a human.

## Shipping fee
The shipping fee is refunded only when the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent. It is not refunded when the customer changed their mind.

## Non-refundable items
Gift cards and digital downloads cannot be refunded.

## Large refunds
Refunds above 5000 rupees need approval from a human before any money moves. The agent files the request and tells the customer it is awaiting approval.

## Undelivered orders
Orders that have not been delivered yet cannot be refunded by the agent. Escalate to a human, who can contact the courier.
